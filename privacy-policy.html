We may collect information such as:

  • Age
  • Gender

This information may be used to personalize your analysis, recommendations, and grooming routines.

Usage and App Data

We may process information about how you use the App, including:

If you purchase a premium subscription, payment is processed through Google Play. Dapper AI does not directly collect or store your credit-card, debit-card, or other payment-card details. Use of Face Data

Your facial photographs are processed to provide the facial analysis and grooming-related features of Dapper AI. We do not sell, rent, or provide your facial photographs to advertisers or data brokers. We do not use your facial photographs for advertising purposes.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Local Storage on Your Device

Your scan photographs may be stored locally on your device so that you can view your scan history and track before/after progress. Locally stored photographs remain on your device until you delete them through the App or otherwise remove them from your device.

Cloud Storage

A copy of a scan photograph may also be temporarily stored on our cloud infrastructure, including Firebase, for purposes such as providing App functionality, technical support, troubleshooting, and quality assurance. Cloud-stored scan photographs are automatically scheduled for permanent deletion after 7 days, subject to technical limitations or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

During this limited retention period, authorized members of our team may have restricted access to the data when reasonably necessary for support, troubleshooting, debugging, or quality-assurance purposes. We do not retain cloud copies of scan photographs indefinitely.

4. Third-Party AI Services

To provide facial analysis and personalized recommendations, your facial photographs may be transmitted to Google's Gemini API, a third-party artificial intelligence service. The photographs are transmitted for the purpose of processing the requested analysis and generating relevant results.

Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. You should review Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation for information about how data submitted to its services is handled.

Dapper AI does not sell, rent, or share your facial photographs with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through Dapper AI against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic transmission, storage, or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete locally stored scan data through the App where the relevant functionality is available.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated cloud data by contacting us.
  • Request information about personal data associated with your use of Dapper AI.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate personal information.
